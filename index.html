ENGINEERING CAPABILITIES

Built for Offline Integrity & Scale

Everything college event organizers, IEEE branches, hackathon coordinators, and educational institutions need.

100% Offline-First

No cloud accounts, third-party APIs, or internet connections required. Zero tracking, ensuring absolute privacy for participant data and records.

Excel & CSV Roster Import

Imports spreadsheets with dynamic header auto-detection, schema mapping, duplicate detection, and full UTF-8 Unicode support (Malayalam, Hindi, Tamil, etc.).

Ed25519 Cryptographic Signatures

Every certificate is signed with high-security Ed25519 digital signatures. Any tampering with participant name, issue date, or event details immediately invalidates the seal.

Vector PDF & Offline QR

Generates high-DPI vector PDFs ready for professional printing. Includes embedded QR codes encoding canonical cryptographic verification payloads.

Batch ZIP Generation

Batch generate hundreds of certificates in seconds with individual PDF files bundled into an organized, compressed `.zip` archive ready for distribution.

Tamper-Evident Audit Logs

Every creation, signing event, and export is recorded in an immutable, cryptographically hash-chained SHA-256 audit ledger to prevent unauthorized alterations.
